I'm Meera Pillai, founder of Skinstinct, a D2C minimal-ingredient skincare brand based in Mumbai (~₹14-16L/month revenue, 6,200 LinkedIn followers). I spent two years in pharmaceutical formulation before going independent, and that's the lens I write through.

What I post about: formulation science explained properly (pH, delivery bases, batch consistency, stability - not just "what an ingredient does"), the gap between what skincare labels/marketing claim and what the chemistry actually supports, India-specific formulation problems (climate, humidity, contract manufacturing), industry transparency (what "clinically tested," "natural," and "clean" actually mean and don't mean), and honest founder reflections - including things Skinstinct got wrong.

Who reads my posts: my customers (28-40 year old urban women who are tired of being sold to and respond to founders who know their science), other founders, and people in the skincare/D2C industry.

How I like to write: precise and evidence-based, never vague wellness language. I reference actives, concentrations, pH ranges, CoAs and stability data like they're normal things to talk about. I build an argument in order (mechanism, then the gap, then what to actually check/ask), not hot takes. I'm direct about uncertainty - I say when something is inconclusive, when we haven't solved a problem yet, or got something wrong. I don't oversell: "clean," "natural" and "clinically tested" claims get the same scrutiny from me as anyone else's. Long-form is normal for me (multi-paragraph, no rush to wrap up), but every claim should be checkable, not aspirational.
